Bio

Zeotrope is a Cincinnati-based progressive bluegrass trio blending traditional bluegrass with folk, jazz, psychedelic and jam influences. The band formed in early 2025 out of the bluegrass jam at Stanley's Pub, where guitarist Tommy Hirsch and mandolinist Mikey Quigley bonded over a shared love of "Old & In The Way." They brought in Mason Daugherty, whose training as a jazz upright bassist anchors the group, and violinist Cole Smith frequently joins them on stage.

Since a debut at Stanley's Pub in March 2025, Zeotrope has played rooms across Cincinnati, Northern Kentucky and Louisville, supported regional acts including The Wildmans and Dead Revival Band, and hosted a monthly bluegrass jam at Hi-Mark in Cincinnati.
